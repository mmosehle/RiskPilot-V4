News Release Engine
Anticipate the likely directional bias of an upcoming USD news release — before it prints. Answer a few context questions and the engine scores the probability of a bullish or bearish USD reaction based on current Fed expectations, recent data trend, and market positioning signals.
💡
How this differs from the Direction Engine: The Direction Engine analyses a release after the actual figure is known. This engine anticipates the likely bias before the release — based on the conditions that historically shape the market's reaction. Use both together for a complete fundamental picture.

⚠️
Pre-release bias — not a trade signal
This engine models the conditions that historically shape the market's reaction. It does not predict the actual figure. A strong pre-release bullish bias can instantly reverse if the actual data misses. Always wait for the release, confirm with the Direction Engine, and look for a technical setup before entering. Never trade the spike — trade the confirmation.
